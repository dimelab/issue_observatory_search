ForceAtlas2 Settings

The Layout switch in the toolbar sets Strong gravity, Gravity, Scaling and Keep small components near: Compact is the default; Organic follows the shape of the data. Changing one of them yourself shows Custom.

On (Compact): gravity grows with the distance from the centre, which keeps everything close and filling the screen but makes every network round; lower Gravity (about 0.05 is a gentle value) for a looser result. Off (Organic, as in Gephi): gravity has a constant strength, so the layout takes the shape of the data (chains stay chains, clusters stay apart).

Separate pieces of the network (for example a few sites that share one keyword) are held just outside the main cluster (on in Organic). Off: as in Gephi, they drift far out and make the main cluster small; with strong gravity they stay near anyway.
